6:00 to 10:00 pmReception at the Sweetgrass Pavilion

6:00 pmGuests are invited to walk the stone path to the Pavilion first. Tables are listed on the mirror seating chart at the entrance. Everyone being introduced hangs back with a drink and is walked over by Dana and lined up outside the Pavilion.
About 6:15Grand entrance, introduced by Jony (the MC), in this order: groomsmen as a group, bridesmaids as a group, Kristine's parents, Julian's mom, flower granny and ring bearers together, then Kristine and Julian.
Right afterFirst dance. Then parent dances: Kristine with her dad for the first half of the song and her mom for the second half, then Julian with his mom.
Right afterToasts, all together before the buffet opens. Bernie gives the welcome, then the two best men and the two maids of honor (order to be settled with the four speakers at the rehearsal dinner). Julian then gives a short toast to his mom, and Kristine closes with a very brief thank you from the two of them before the party starts. Shooting for under 4 minutes each, ideally. The bar stays open the whole time.
About 6:45Buffet opens, table by table. Everything is labeled, including gluten free and dairy free. Plates are at the buffet, not on the tables.
Around sunsetKristine and Julian slip out for portraits. They will be back in about 15 minutes.
After dinnerDance floor opens. Dessert is a Viennese table plus an ice cream sundae bar, and s'mores at the fire pits on the patio. Photo booth is at the top of the ramp by the card box. Lawn games are on the patio.
9:55 pmLast song. Dana will invite everyone to head to the pool so Kristine and Julian can have a private last dance.
10:00 pmReception ends. Walk or golf cart back to the hotel.

10:00 pm to 1:00 amAfter party at the indoor pool

  • Wristbands at the door. Invited guests only. The pool, hot tubs, spa and sauna are private to the wedding group. The outdoor pool is closed.
  • Towels come from guest rooms. The pool does not stock them and the front desk will not announce it. Swimsuits are welcome; so are dry clothes.
  • Food goes out at 10:30 pm: spinach and artichoke dip, mac and cheese bites, wings, churros.
  • Open bar 10:30 pm to midnight, then cash bar. Last call 1:00 am. Tips for the bartenders are appreciated.
  • Lifeguards and security are on duty. If there is lightning, everyone is out of the water for 30 minutes; the bar and sauna stay open and they usually extend the end time.
  • Floats are already at the pool. No glass on the deck.
